# Voice Profile — The Luna Who Said No

> From novel-skills' ns-voice-style-analyser. The book's voice, quantified.
> Every chapter must match this profile. Drift = rewrite.

## Book Voice

- **Sentence length**: predominantly short (3–12 words). Long sentences (15–25 words) reserved for emotional peaks and atmospheric stretches. Never stack 3+ long sentences.
- **Diction**: simple, Anglo-Saxon, physical. No Latinate abstraction (no "utilize," "facilitate," "consequently").
- **Tone**: dark / intimate / unsentimental. Humor is dry and barbed, never slapstick.
- **POV**: close third (Elara). Psychic distance stays tight — we feel her body before we hear her thoughts.
- **Sensory preference**: smell > sound > touch > sight. (Werewolf senses. Show the world through nose and ears first.)
- **Punctuation**: em dashes used sparingly (≤2 per chapter) for genuine interruption. No semicolon showing off.

## Character Language Markers (≥3 each — the blind voice test)

### Elara Voss
1. **Barbed brevity**: short sentences, rhetorical questions as weapons ("Important?"). Never explains herself twice.
2. **Clinical register under stress**: ER-nurse vocabulary leaks in (assess, triage, pressure, steady) when cornered.
3. **Banned from her mouth**: flowery metaphor, self-pity monologues, "I feel so..." constructions.
4. **Sensory**: notices smells first, always.

### Kael Blackthorn
1. **Terse**: fewest words in any room. Statements, not explanations. Lets silence do the work.
2. **No filler verbs**: never "sort of," "kind of," "I guess." When uncertain, he goes quiet instead of hedging.
3. **Physical speech beats**: his dialogue is carried by what his body does while not talking.
4. **Banned from his mouth**: long emotional speeches (until the dam breaks — max once per act), sarcasm (that's Elara's weapon, not his).

### Selene
1. **Honeyed cruelty**: compliments that cut ("How brave of you to show your face"). Always polite on the surface.
2. **Third-person distance**: talks about people as pieces ("the girl," "our little nurse") to diminish them.
3. **Never**: raises her voice, uses crude insults, snarls. Her menace is in the smile.
