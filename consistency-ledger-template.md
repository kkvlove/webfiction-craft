## Character Dossiers

> Framework: Wound → Lie → Want → Need (from novel-skills' character-designer).

### [Protagonist Name] ([age], [role])

- **Lie they believe**: The false belief the arc will break.
- **Want** (external): The plot goal.
- **Need** (internal, conflicts with Want): The truth they must accept.
