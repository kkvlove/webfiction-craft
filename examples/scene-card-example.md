# Scene Card — Chapter 13 "The Second Page"

**Goal**: Elara moves the diary to a hiding place nobody will check — and discovers the theft was worse than she knew. Kael tracks her (the bond pulling him), and for the first time she lets him guard a secret without telling him the contents.

**Conflict**: She must cross the pack house with the diary on her person while her body betrays her — the reforming bond sharpens her senses and makes her wolf restless. Kael confronts her in the shrine: he smelled her, followed her, demands what she's hiding.

**Turning Point**: Mid-confrontation the bond surges — two-way. He felt it from two floors down. Instead of pressing her, he offers the one thing she can't refuse: "Whatever's in that wall, I'll stand outside the door." She tells him the diary exists. Not its contents. But its existence.

**Emotional Value Turn**: Guarded −1 → Exposed −3 (the surge, his stillness, almost telling) → Resolved +1 (diary hidden, Kael as guard) → Dread −2 (the second torn page)

**Outcome**: Entry: Elara trusts no one with the diary; the bond is her private war. Exit: the diary is hidden in the east shrine's altar base, Kael knows it exists and has volunteered as its guard — and Elara has found proof that a second page was already torn out before the room search she noticed. She doesn't know what's missing.
